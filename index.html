About me

Hi there, I'm Brooke

I'm an avid world traveler who has visited 70+ countries — though I call NYC home when I'm not on the road. I especially love getting off the beaten path and losing myself in nature, and I'm particularly passionate about solo female travel, especially for women in their 30's. I use my creative lens to help other travelers find unique stays and magical oases around the world.

I partner with hotels, Airbnbs, resorts, brands and tourism boards to help encourage people to travel and explore unique, exciting destinations where they would be happy to get stuck and stay awhile. If you'd like to work together, I'd love to hear from you.
